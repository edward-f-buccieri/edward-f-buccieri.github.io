About me
======
I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from the University of Pavia and currently work as a Researcher at the Centre for Inherited Cardiovascular Diseases (Policlinico San Matteo, Pavia). Since 2024, I have been developing Deep Learning models applied to Computational Pathology, specifically for the identification of acute cellular rejection in heart transplant patients.

I recently completed my master's thesis on Gradient Flows and Optimal Transport in Neural Network Training. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities in Europe to further explore Optimal Transport, Wasserstein Gradient Flows, and the mathematical foundations of Machine Learning.
